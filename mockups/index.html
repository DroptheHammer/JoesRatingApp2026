How it works

  1. The Quest: all the Original Joe's locations, with visited ones showing their score badge.
  2. Rate at the Table: each person taps 1–10 for the 5 categories, adds comments and photos. The avatars show who's finished.
  3. The Wrap-Up: once everyone submits, the app shows each category's average, everyone's scores side by side, and the weighted overall score.
  4. Report Card: the permanent record for that visit, with letter grades, photos and everyone's comments.
  5. Leaderboard: ranks every Joe's, overall or by a single category.

Scoring: overall = 40% Food + 20% Decor + 15% Service + 15% Bathroom + 10% Christmas Spirit (the tertiary category), using the group average for each category. Letter grades: 9+ A, 8+ B, 7+ C, 6+ D. Try tapping the numbers on screen 2. The score updates live.

Placeholders: the location list, family names and all scores are sample data.
